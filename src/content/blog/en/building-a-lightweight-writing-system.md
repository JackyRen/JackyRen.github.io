The most important feature of a personal blog is not a theme switcher, search, or comments. It is making the writer want to keep writing.

## Lower the publishing friction first

A good writing system should make a new post simple: create Markdown, fill in frontmatter, write, commit. Pages, archives, and deployment should happen automatically.

## Add power gradually

After the writing habit is stable, add tags, search, RSS, or comments. Otherwise, every feature increases maintenance cost before the site has enough content to justify it.

## Keep it portable

Markdown content and static output keep the site easy to move later, whether it stays on GitHub Pages, moves to Cloudflare Pages, or runs on another host.
